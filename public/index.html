Settings

Your trigger

Say "oi, oi, oi" with a short break between each "oi". The app listens silently with a Spanish speech recognizer that runs on the phone itself: no beeps, no internet needed, and nothing is sent anywhere. The first time, it downloads the recognizer (about 35 MB); after that it is kept on the phone.

Heard –
Say "oi, oi, oi"

Test mode never creates flags. "Heard" shows what the recognizer understood; "hoy" counts as "oi" because they sound the same. Then talk normally or play the radio to check it stays quiet.

Your details for reports

Kept only on this phone. Used to fill in the claim report you send to the authorities.

Share the map

KML opens in Google My Maps and Google Earth. GeoJSON loads in QGIS, ArcGIS and most public GIS tools.
