Your trigger

Record the shout you want to use, ideally 3 times, with the phone where it sits while you drive. OIOIOI then listens for a sound that is much louder than the background and shaped like your shout. You can also say a word like "hoyo", "pothole" or "oioioi".

Shout now

Test mode never creates flags. For a word, "Heard" shows what the phone understood. For a shout, the bar must pass the white line and "Shape match" must turn green for long enough. Practice until you see "Recognized" every time, then talk or play the radio to check it stays quiet.
